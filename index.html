Aro Terms and Conditions

1. Multi-Store Commercial Scope & Legalities

Aro operates four commercial store formats with distinct transaction policies:

  • AroMarket: Standard commercial retail with verified consumer warranty rights and a standard 30-day return policy for eligible new merchandise.
  • Thrift: One-of-a-kind vintage and circular goods sold with verified condition grades, precise garment measurements, mandatory smoke/pet allergen declarations, and a 72-hour buyer inspection window for Item Not As Described (INAD) claims.
  • Auction: Legally binding purchase contracts under commercial auction law with mandatory payment method pre-authorization, anti-sniping 2-minute countdown extensions, and a strict 10-minute purchase settlement window before automatic forfeiture to the second highest bidder.
  • Art: Original handcrafted artisan goods, custom milestone commission contracts, digital asset source licensing, and 100% tip pass-through to creators with 0% platform fee.

2. Platform Guarantee, Seller Warranties & Fund Forfeiture

Sellers act as independent commercial merchants or curators and are legally, strictly, and solely responsible for all merchandise they list or sell:

  • Platform Guarantee & Seller Warranties: Aro does not check or verify authenticity; Aro guarantees that buyers receive what they requested or their money back in full under the 72-hour buyer inspection window. Sellers warrant that they possess full legal ownership and lawful authority to sell every item, and must ship the exact item requested. If a seller fails to deliver what was requested, the buyer gets their money back and the seller forfeits funds. Once delivered as requested without dispute within 72 hours, the buyer forfeits the funds to the seller.
  • Product Safety & Legal Liability: Sellers are strictly liable for the safety, merchantability, and consumer regulatory compliance of their products. Sellers are financially and legally liable for any damages, defects, or injuries arising from their goods.
  • Disclosure Accuracy: Falsely declaring an item as Smoke-Free or Pet-Free when exposure occurred is considered material misrepresentation (INAD), resulting in an immediate full refund charged back to the seller.
  • Packaging Standards: Sellers must package goods to withstand commercial transit. Damage caused by inadequate packing is the seller's sole financial liability. Deliberate postage weight misdeclarations exceeding 20% are subject to carrier penalty chargebacks.
  • Seller Indemnification: Sellers agree to indemnify and hold harmless Aro and its buyers from any third-party claims, liabilities, or legal fees resulting from their listings, products, or regulatory violations.

3. Platform Fee Structure & Platform-Funded Free Shipping

  • Standard 15% Platform Fee: A flat 15% commission fee applies to all completed marketplace sales (sellers receive 85% net payout).
  • Platform-Funded Free Shipping Under 1 lb: All orders weighing under 1.0 lb (16 oz), as well as digital deliveries, receive automatic Free Shipping for buyers, funded directly out of the 15% platform fee with $0 surcharge to the buyer or seller.
  • Orders Over 1 lb: Standard commercial parcel postage rates apply based on actual carrier package weight and zone.
  • 0% Fee on Artisan Tips: 100% of buyer tips pass through directly to artisan creators without deduction.

4. In-House Store Credit Programs & Community Rewards

  • Thrift Donation 5% Storage Dividend: Donating pre-loved circular items awards an immediate $5.00 welcome credit upon acceptance. Upon sale, 5% of gross sale price is automatically returned directly to the donor as In-House Aro Store Credit (Storage Dividend). The remaining 95% funds shipping logistics, FAQ community rewards, and archive storage.
  • Community FAQ Rewards ($5.00): Answering customer inquiries in the Aro FAQ that achieve 3 community endorsements/upvotes automatically grants $5.00 in Aro Store Credits.
  • Referral Program ($20 Referrer / $10 Referee): Inviting friends grants $20 to the referrer and $10 to the referee as in-house promotional store credit upon verified mobile carrier registration.
  • Credit Terms of Use: All credits are non-cashable in-house promotional store credits with zero cash surrender value. Credits are spendable at a 1:1 USD valuation across AroMarket, Thrift, Auction, and Art stores at checkout.

5. 72-Hour Buyer Protection & Delayed Payouts

All buyer payments are held securely during a 72-hour (3 calendar day) inspection window commencing upon confirmed carrier delivery. Funds automatically disburse to the seller upon window expiration if no dispute is opened. In the event of a dispute, funds remain frozen until resolved by the Dispute Arbitration Desk.

6. Strict Prohibition of Mystery Boxes & Gambling

Mystery crates, blind boxes, pack breaks, and random chance lotteries are strictly prohibited across all stores in compliance with commercial consumer protection, sweepstakes, and anti-gambling regulations.

7. Tax Compliance (Marketplace Facilitator) & Mobile Verification

Aro automatically collects and remits state and local sales tax under statutory Marketplace Facilitator Laws. Sellers meeting statutory thresholds must provide verified tax information for 1099-K reporting. Physical mobile carrier SMS verification is required to prevent bot automation; VoIP and virtual numbers are filtered upon submission.
